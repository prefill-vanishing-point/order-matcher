      How each line is decided

      1. Start with what this restaurant buys

        Their last 90 days of purchases become the options, because most lines are reorders. "None of these" is always an option too.

      2. Look wider when unsure

        Below 0.85 confidence, or on "none of these", it searches what every restaurant has bought recently and asks again.

      3. Keep the chef's unit

        If the order says case, lb or each, the line uses that unit. With no unit, it uses the one this restaurant usually orders.

      4. Flag what needs a person

        Lines under 0.90 confidence wait for someone to confirm or change them. Everything else is ready to send.
